Adaptive study for children, with a report for the guardian

A study app that generates questions with AI and adjusts difficulty in real time as the student answers, with a practice mode and an exam mode, plus a topic-mastery report for whoever is following along.

Education Adaptive Firebase
3 pedagogical agents
2 modes: practice and exam
0 redeploys needed to switch model

The problem

Printed study material has one level of difficulty. A child who already knows the topic repeats what they know, and one who does not gets stuck at the same point with nobody to explain it differently. The person following along, meanwhile, sees the grade — not where the gap is.

The solution

Three agents with distinct roles, and the adaptive logic deliberately outside the LLM.

  • Pedagogical engineer — generates the question contextualised to the student profile, multiple choice or free text.
  • Pedagogical corrector — evaluates the answer in two modes: tutor, with Socratic guidance, or examiner, with a grade.
  • Report summariser — writes the natural-language summary for the guardian, with caching so no call repeats.

Topic mastery is an exponential moving average weighted by difficulty, in ordinary testable code. So is the adaptation: two correct answers in a row raise the difficulty, one wrong answer lowers it, and the next topic is always the weakest one. Keeping this outside the model makes the behaviour predictable and auditable — and cheap.

The AI provider and model are configured through an admin panel, stored in a collection the client cannot read. Switching from Gemini to Claude requires no redeploy.

Outcome

The guardian stopped seeing only a grade and started seeing mastery by topic, with a revision session built from the weakest areas. And switching models became configuration, which turned the app into a practical bench for comparing providers.
