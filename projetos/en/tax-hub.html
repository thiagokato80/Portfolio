Tax-Hub

Tax simulation for interstate pricing in Brazil

A simulator that prices sales between Brazilian states applying ICMS-ST, DIFAL, FCP, IPI and PIS/COFINS, with an auditable calculation trail and formulas the tax analyst registers directly — no deploy required.

Tax Monorepo Supabase
171 automated tests
5 taxes in the pipeline
4 simulation modes

The problem

Pricing a sale between two Brazilian states is not arithmetic — it is a combination of rules that changes by route, by product code and by the tax regime of each party. ICMS-ST, DIFAL, FCP, IPI and PIS/COFINS interact, and the order in which they enter the calculation changes the result.

The aggravating factor is not the complexity: it is the rate of change. A new state rule means a new formula. When the formula lives in code, every legislative change becomes a development task, and the tax analyst — the person who actually knows the rule — depends on a deploy queue.

The solution

The decision that shaped the architecture was taking the formula out of the code: new formulas, with new variables, are registered inside the application. The analyst writes the formula, tests it against known-good golden cases, and publishes — with no developer in the loop.

  • Pure calculation engine in TypeScript, with no I/O and no framework dependency. Decimal-precision maths via decimal.js.
  • Custom expression language — hand-written tokeniser, parser and evaluator, with no eval. A user-registered formula never becomes blindly executed code.
  • Step-by-step calculation trail, exportable to Excel with live formulas rather than static values, or printed as a formal audit document.
  • Reverse solver by bisection, for target-price and target-margin modes.
  • Fiscal rule resolver by scope, specificity and validity window.

The front end talks directly to Supabase from the browser, with Row Level Security in the database. There is no custom backend server — a deliberate choice, documented in the architecture.

Outcome

A change in tax rules stopped being an engineering request. The people who understand tax now change the system directly, with a safety net: the golden cases fail before publication if a new formula breaks a known case.

Every simulation carries its own audit trail — the number never appears alone, always with the calculation that produced it.
