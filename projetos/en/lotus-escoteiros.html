Lótus Scout Group Management System

Financial and educational management for a volunteer organisation

A complete platform for a 460-member scout group — membership fees with discount rules, reimbursement workflow with approval tiers, accounting close and youth progression tracking — running at zero cost.

The problem

A scout group of 460 people has the administrative complexity of a small company — fees with sibling discounts, reimbursements needing approval, accountability to a fiscal council — and the budget of a non-profit run by volunteers.

The real constraint was not technical but economic: any solution with a recurring subscription was out. And manual processes consumed the time of people who were already donating their time.

The solution

I acted as technical lead and full stack developer — from requirements gathering and the specification through to data modelling, security architecture and infrastructure.

Every technology choice was guided by an unusual criterion: strong performance within free tiers.

  • Automated billing with compound rules — sibling discounts distributed across cost centres, board and leadership discounts.
  • Reimbursement workflow with approval tiers by amount and auditing with attached receipts.
  • Monthly close with locking: a month approved by the fiscal council can no longer be edited, which guarantees accounting integrity.
  • Unified progression — activities, badges and achievements, with a real-time approval queue for leaders and parents.
  • Granular permissions by profile, isolated in the database through Row Level Security.
  • Keep-alive to prevent the free database instance from auto-pausing — the mitigation that makes the free tier viable in production.

The interface is mobile-first for a concrete reason: section leaders record progression and approve reimbursements in the field, from their phones.

Outcome

Zero monthly running cost at the group's current scale, with processes that used to be manual now running on their own. Moving between development and production is transparent, through environment variables and SQL migrations.
