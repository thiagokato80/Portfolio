Multi-Agent Assistant

Operations copilot across ERP, email and Microsoft Teams

A multi-agent assistant that unifies scattered data sources — ERP, cloud storage and email — and answers in natural language inside Teams, with a plan-and-act loop that resolves multi-step tasks.

Agents RAG Integration
3 domains: CRM, PMO and help desk
1 interface: natural language

The problem

The information that answers a management question almost never lives in a single system. The order is in the ERP, the attachment is in cloud storage, the agreement is in an email, and the decision has to happen now — in Teams, where the team already is.

The cost is not the time spent opening three screens: it is the broken train of thought. Someone who has to switch systems to finish a thought often does not finish it.

The solution

An assistant that converses in natural language inside Teams and fetches data wherever it lives, without requiring the user to know where it came from.

  • Domain specialist agents — CRM, projects and support — each with the vocabulary and rules of its own area.
  • Plan-and-act loop: for a question requiring several steps, the orchestrator builds the plan before executing, rather than trying to answer in one shot.
  • Direct ERP reads via API, in real time, rather than over a stale copy.
  • Document retrieval (RAG) for everything that is not structured data.

Outcome

Questions that used to require opening three systems became answerable in the conversation the team was already in. The assistant became the first stop for operational queries.

Real use also exposed the limit of a specialist-agent architecture: depth within each area, but analytical silos between them. Questions that cross domains need a data federation layer, which is the evolution mapped for the project.
