First vertical in development

LexMind

Legal agents for public tenders and government contracting

A multi-tenant platform of legal agents that research, analyse tender documents and draft filings — always anchored in a real source with verified validity, and with each citation resolved against the document at answer time.

RAG pgvector Multi-tenant
768 dimensions per embedding
2 models, one per task
3 test layers: unit, RLS and E2E

The problem

A legal assistant that invents case law is worse than no assistant at all: the error arrives looking like a finished answer, and finding out costs a great deal. In public tenders, where the governing statute coexists with shifting regulations, checking whether a rule is still in force is part of the work — not a detail.

There is also the problem of what has already been done: filings, arguments and previous tender analyses sit scattered in folders, and the firm's knowledge is not searchable.

The solution

The platform was designed around three explicit guarantees, and the architecture exists to uphold them.

  • It never hallucinates — every statement from the copilot carries a [source:chunk_id], resolved in real time against verified documents. No source, no statement.
  • It never forgets — filings, arguments and tender documents become searchable institutional memory, isolated per firm.
  • It never sleeps — the analyst agent reads the full tender document and delivers a structured diagnosis (qualification, deadlines, restrictive clauses, contradictions) before the lawyer opens the file.

Search is hybrid — vector plus full text, combined via RRF — because exact legal terms and semantic similarity fail in opposite situations. Two models split the work by affinity: Claude Sonnet for drafting and the copilot, Gemini 2.5 Pro for tender analysis and OCR.

Isolation between firms is tested as code: there is a dedicated test layer for the Row Level Security policies.

Outcome

Reading a tender document stopped being the lawyer's first task and became the review of an already structured diagnosis. And every copilot answer can be checked against its source in one click — which changes the relationship of trust with the tool.
