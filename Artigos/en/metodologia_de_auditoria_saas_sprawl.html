"Sovereign Audit" Methodology: mapping SaaS sprawl

A five-phase roadmap to discover how much unmonitored software a company pays for, measure the effect of AI on per-seat licensing, and decide with technical criteria what to repatriate.

March 30, 2026 · 2 min read

In this article

  • 1. Discovery and visibility mapping (month 1)
  • 2. Efficiency analysis and seat compression
  • 3. Decision matrix: repatriate or keep
  • 4. ROI projection and business case
  • 5. "Vibe &amp; Verify" implementation roadmap

1. Discovery and visibility mapping (month 1)

The initial goal is to eliminate the operational blindness caused by the proliferation of unmonitored software.

  • Shadow IT mapping: identifying applications that account for 30% to 40% of the corporate ecosystem, generating unmonitored costs of up to US$ 5 million a year.
  • Functional inventory: categorising tools by function — collaboration, analytics, CRM, HR — to identify overlaps.
  • Inactivity diagnosis: identifying underused licences, given that waste in collaboration tools can reach 58%.

2. Efficiency analysis and seat compression

This phase assesses the impact of AI on the traditional licensing model.

  • Seat compression calculation: assessing departments where AI agents have reduced the need for humans — reductions of up to 81% in support and 92% in data entry.
  • Value conflict identification: finding tools where the vendor anchors AI features in premium tiers to offset the loss of users, pushing waste to 47%.
  • Quantifying the financial drain: estimating the direct annual loss based on the market average, around US$ 18 million for large enterprises.

3. Decision matrix: repatriate or keep

Using technical criteria to define what should become a proprietary asset.

  • Defining core systems: selecting for repatriation the systems that define competitive advantage — risk, logistics and pricing algorithms.
  • Data sovereignty analysis: assessing the need for full control and compliance with sovereign clouds, a market growing 35.6% a year.

4. ROI projection and business case

Presenting the financial viability of the transition to the board.

  • Repatriation ROI: demonstrating a return in under 24 months.
  • FinOps strategy: planning the replacement of fixed licensing costs with variable, optimisable infrastructure costs.

5. "Vibe &amp; Verify" implementation roadmap

The final deliverable of the audit includes the AI-assisted execution plan.

  • Prototyping with vibe coding: using AppGen tools to create MVPs in weeks, cutting engineering cost by 60%.
  • Automatic verification layer: infrastructure to control technical debt — which grows three times faster in AI-generated code — and guarantee security.
